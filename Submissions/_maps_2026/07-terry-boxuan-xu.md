A minimal street portrait of central Philadelphia in navy, blue, and gold. Six named corridors receive gold accents. Colors evoke night and do not measure illumination or activity. Sources — City of Philadelphia street centerlines and Philadelphia Water Department hydrography. The assisted map build was timed after data preparation.
