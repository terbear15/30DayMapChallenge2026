A terrain viewshed from the north summit of Twin Peaks in San Francisco. Amber shows land visible at an eye height of 1.7 metres. Two terrain profiles show a clear view and a view blocked by a ridge. The model uses USGS 3DEP elevation and DataSF street centerlines and shoreline. Buildings, trees, and weather are outside the model.
