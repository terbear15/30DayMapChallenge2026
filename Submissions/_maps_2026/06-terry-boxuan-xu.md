Philadelphia during its 1790–1800 decade as the U.S. capital, shown on John Hills's 1796 plan. A larger district inset locates Congress Hall, Independence Hall, the President's House, and Old City Hall. The original engraving supplies the street geometry and period labels. New annotations and a timeline explain the buildings' roles.

Historical map credit — Library of Congress, Geography and Map Division. Landmark histories — National Park Service, Federal Judicial Center, and U.S. Supreme Court.
