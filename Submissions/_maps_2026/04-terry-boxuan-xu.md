Manhattan's mapped café clusters, with an island overview and three close-ups. Terracotta identifies cafés assigned to nearby groups; gray shows mapped cafés outside clusters. The clustering uses a 140-metre neighborhood and minPts=5. Sources: OpenStreetMap contributors and NYC Department of City Planning. The map is an exploratory snapshot with incomplete coverage, not a ranking of cafés.
